# 02 · Casa (sala y cocina)

**Para qué módulos:** familia, partes de la casa y habitaciones, rutina diaria, comida y bebida, tiempo libre, clima.
**Archivos a guardar:** `casa_back.png` y `casa_front.png`.
**Luz:** ventana grande a la **izquierda**, luz de tarde cálida.

Pega antes de cada prompt el **bloque de estilo** de `00_estilo_comun.md`.

## ESCENA COMPLETA (paso 1)
```
A cozy open-plan living room and kitchen seen from chest height. Left: a large window with warm afternoon light and light
curtains, a comfy sofa with cushions below a few framed abstract pictures (no readable content), a wooden bookshelf with
books and plants, a soft rug on a wooden floor. Back wall: warm beige, a hanging lamp. In the foreground a kitchen island
counter across the bottom of the frame with a fruit bowl, two cups and a small herb pot. The center-right area behind the
counter is calm and uncluttered (a plain warm wall).
```

## BACK (paso 2)
```
Same image, remove the kitchen island counter and everything on it; fill the floor and wall behind it plausibly. Keep
everything else exactly identical: same camera, window, sofa, shelf, rug, lamp and colors.
```

## FRONT (paso 3)
```
Keep ONLY the kitchen island counter with the fruit bowl, two cups and herb pot, on a solid pure green (#00FF00)
background. Do not change its size, position, outline or colors. Nothing else in the image.
```

## Notas
- Mantén el sofá y la estantería a la **izquierda** y deja libre la derecha.
- Colores cálidos (madera, beige, verde suave): contrastan bien con la camisa blanca del profesor.
