# 01 · Aula con pizarra

**Para qué módulos:** alfabeto y ortografía, pronombres, números, días y meses, verbos, preguntas básicas. Es el escenario «base» (el más usado).
**Archivos a guardar:** `aula_back.png` y `aula_front.png`.
**Luz:** ventana a la **izquierda**, luz de mañana blanca y cálida.

Pega antes de cada prompt el **bloque de estilo** de `00_estilo_comun.md`.

## ESCENA COMPLETA (paso 1)
```
A bright modern classroom seen from chest height. On the left wall a big window with daylight and a few green plants on the
sill. Back wall: a large clean white whiteboard (completely blank, no writing) with a marker tray, a framed world map and
two colorful abstract posters on the sides. Wooden floor, a bookshelf with colorful books in the far left corner, a few
student chairs stacked neatly at the far left. In the foreground a wooden teacher's desk across the bottom of the frame
with a neat stack of books, a mug, a small potted plant and a pen holder. The center-right area behind the desk is calm
and uncluttered (a plain light wall and part of the whiteboard).
```

## BACK (paso 2)
```
Same image, remove the teacher's desk and every object in the foreground; fill the floor and wall behind it plausibly.
Keep everything else exactly identical: same camera, window, whiteboard, map, posters, shelves, colors.
```

## FRONT (paso 3)
```
Keep ONLY the wooden teacher's desk with the stack of books, mug, potted plant and pen holder, on a solid pure green
(#00FF00) background. Do not change its size, position, outline or colors. Nothing else in the image.
```

## Notas
- La pizarra se deja **en blanco** a propósito: más adelante se le pueden poner letras o ejemplos encima.
- Si la mesa sale muy alta, que no tape más de la cintura del profesor (el borde de la mesa hacia el 78–85 % del alto).
